中文

Penglai is a desktop distribution of official DeepSeek Harness. The last public download remains v0.5.6. 0.5.7 is a development candidate; installer hashes are filled after public readback.

Messaging

The 0.5.7 candidate offers nine connection entries under one Messaging plugin. Public “supported” claims follow the live evidence matrix only; platforms without a live row are not fully supported.

  • Weixin — official iLink QR
  • Feishu — official app QR / credentials
  • DingTalk — device-registration QR + Stream
  • WeCom — intelligent-bot QR
  • QQ — official bot QR, not personal QQ
  • Slack — bot token + app token, no official QR
  • Telegram — bot token long-poll, no official QR
  • Discord — bot token + Gateway, no official QR
  • WhatsApp — experimental community protocol, default off

Not nine fake QR codes

Users see one Messaging plugin. Weixin, Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, QQ, and WhatsApp start a real vendor QR or device-link flow where that protocol actually exists. Slack, Telegram, and Discord honestly use their official manifest or token paths. A visible QR is not a support claim: connection confirmation, private binding, restart recovery, and logout cleanup must still pass.

More than Messaging

0.5.7 also completes Office document workflows, Memory candidates and permissions, Companion, plugin install and rollback, Windows lifecycle behavior, and privacy gates. It is not an IM reskin.
