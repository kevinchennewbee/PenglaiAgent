If you can send a message, you can find your assistant.

中文

Penglai is a desktop distribution of official DeepSeek Harness. The last public download remains v0.5.6. 0.5.7 is a development candidate; installer hashes are filled after public readback.

Messaging

0.5.7 offers eight platform connection entries under one Messaging plugin.

  • Weixin — official iLink QR
  • Feishu — official app QR / credentials
  • DingTalk — device-registration QR + Stream
  • WeCom — intelligent-bot QR
  • QQ — official bot QR, not personal QQ
  • Slack — bot token + app token, no official QR
  • Telegram — bot token long-poll, no official QR
  • Discord — bot token + Gateway, no official QR
  • WhatsApp — community runtime not bundled in 0.5.7

Real connection methods

Users see one Messaging plugin. Weixin, Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, and QQ use their real QR or device-registration flows; Slack, Telegram, and Discord use official manifest or token paths. WhatsApp has no connection action in this release.

More than Messaging

0.5.7 also completes Office document workflows, Memory candidates and permissions, Companion, plugin install and rollback, Windows lifecycle behavior, and privacy gates. It is not an IM reskin.
