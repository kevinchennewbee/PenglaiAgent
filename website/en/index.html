Penglai is a desktop distribution of official DeepSeek Harness. The last public download remains v0.5.6. 0.5.7 is a development candidate; installer hashes are filled after public readback.

Messaging

  • Weixin
  • Feishu
  • DingTalk
  • WeCom
  • QQ
  • Slack
  • Telegram
  • Discord
  • WhatsApp (experimental community protocol, default off)

Support claims follow the live evidence matrix. macOS is ad-hoc and not notarized; Windows has no Authenticode.
